Hairul Anuar Bin Sulaiman

Appointed Islamic Financial Adviser Representative (IFAR)

My practice is built on a singular principle: wealth is a trust (Amanah) that demands disciplined, long-term stewardship. My foundation began in 2013 with a focus on Takaful risk management. As my clients' needs evolved, I expanded into Shariah-compliant investment portfolios in 2017 to provide a multi-dimensional strategy for growth.

In 2021, I made a decisive pivot to become an **Islamic Financial Adviser Representative (IFAR)**. This transition allowed me to adopt a purely advisory-led, **open-architecture framework**, ensuring my commitment remains entirely with my clients' best interests rather than a single provider's catalog.

My vision is to build an institutional-grade firm managing RM100 Million in assets. This goal is a commitment to longevity; it ensures that the systems, technology, and professional standards are in place to serve your family’s wealth continuity for generations to come.

Beyond the portfolios, my approach is rooted in precision. Whether I am cultivating my garden, mastering complex cuisines for family dinners, or training in recurve archery with my son as we chase an Olympic dream, I understand that the best legacies require time, discipline, and a structured plan to hit the mark.
