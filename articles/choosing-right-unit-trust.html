Choosing the Right Unit Trust Funds

Principles for Shariah Compliance and disciplined portfolio construction.

Precision in Selection

Selecting the right Shariah-compliant Unit Trust funds is a blend of financial science and ethical integrity. Beyond just chasing the highest returns, a disciplined investor must evaluate fund objectives, risk profiles, and the quality of Shariah governance. At HAS Wealth, we treat fund selection like archery—it requires a steady hand, a clear target, and a structured process to hit the mark.

The Two-Tier Shariah Screening Process

A Shariah-compliant fund does not simply avoid prohibited industries. To ensure the highest standards of Barakah and compliance, each underlying company must pass a rigorous two-tier filter:

FILTER 01

Business Activity Screening

This qualitative filter excludes companies involved in conventional finance, gambling, alcohol, tobacco, and non-halal food production. It ensures the business model itself is ethical and permissible.

FILTER 02

Financial Ratio Screening

This quantitative filter analyzes debt-to-equity and interest-income ratios. Companies must remain within strict Shariah thresholds (typically below 33%) to prevent over-leverage and excessive interest exposure.

Metrics of Momentum: Risk-Adjusted Returns

While Shariah compliance is the foundation, financial suitability is the driver. We analyze funds based on Risk-Adjusted Returns, looking specifically at how a fund performs during a market downturn. Consistency in the fund management team and the stability of the fund house are metrics we monitor to ensure your "engine" stays reliable.

Alignment with Life Stages

There is no "perfect" fund, only the "right" fund for your specific timeline. Using our **Open-Architecture** advantage through Phillip Mutual, we source funds that align with your current stage of life—whether that is aggressive growth for a child's future or stable income for a dignified retirement.
