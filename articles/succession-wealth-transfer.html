Succession Planning & Wealth Transfer

Ethical and Shariah-compliant strategies to ensure smooth wealth transfer and family continuity.

The Stewardship of Legacy

Effective retirement planning goes beyond personal income; it includes ensuring wealth is transferred seamlessly to future generations. Succession planning within Shariah frameworks preserves family harmony, protects assets from being frozen, and maintains ethical compliance through disciplined **Professional** financial stewardship.

Navigating Shariah Inheritance Laws

While Faraid (Islamic inheritance law) provides the default distribution for a deceased's estate, advanced succession planning allows you to utilize Shariah-compliant tools to manage liquidity and specific legacy goals. This prevents the "Frozen Asset" trap where family members lack the funds to maintain their lifestyle during the lengthy probate process.

📜
PILLAR 01

Hibah Takaful

Providing immediate, tax-free liquidity directly to beneficiaries. This essential tool bypasses the Faraid process, ensuring your loved ones have the financial support they need without probate delays.

🖋️
PILLAR 02

Wasiyah (Will)

The Wasiyah allows for up to 1/3 of your estate to be designated for specific noble causes or non-heirs. This empowers you to fulfill charitable intentions and specific legacy goals within a Shariah framework.

🤝
PILLAR 03

Joint Account Planning

Operational continuity is vital for a surviving spouse. We structure your financial architecture to ensure that the transition period does not compromise your family's daily liquidity or standard of living.

Protecting Your Retirement Comfort

Succession planning does not mean sacrificing your own retirement quality. By using structured investment vehicles and Takaful, you can ensure that you remain financially independent throughout your golden years while simultaneously building the "pipeline" for your children's future stewardship.
