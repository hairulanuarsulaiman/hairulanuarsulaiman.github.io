INSIGHTS HUB / PHASE 03: LEGACY STEWARDSHIP

Building a Sustainable Income Stream in Retirement

Shariah-compliant strategies to ensure reliable and stable income throughout your golden years.

The Shift from Accumulation to Distribution

Creating a sustainable retirement income stream is a distinct challenge from building wealth. While accumulation is about growth, distribution is about **precision**. Shariah-compliant strategies emphasize structured withdrawals and ethical investment choices, providing the Sakinah (peace of mind) needed to maintain your desired lifestyle without the fear of outliving your capital.

The "Bucket Strategy" for Shariah Portfolios

A resilient income stream requires dividing your assets into logical "buckets" based on your time horizon. This prevents you from having to sell growth assets during a temporary market downturn.

💵
BUCKET 01: IMMEDIATE (0-3 YEARS)

Liquidity & Stability

Utilizing Cash and Shariah Money Market funds to cover short-term living expenses. This bucket ensures your day-to-day needs are met regardless of what the stock market is doing.

⚖️
BUCKET 02: INTERMEDIATE (3-7 YEARS)

The Income Engine

Focusing on income-focused Sukuk funds and stable dividend-paying Unit Trusts. This creates a "synthetic salary" that provides predictable cash flow while your long-term assets continue to compound.

🚀
BUCKET 03: GROWTH (7+ YEARS)

Inflation Defense

Allocating Shariah Equities to provide the growth needed to combat long-term inflation. This ensures your purchasing power is protected for your later golden years and future legacy.

Managing the Sequence of Returns

Experiencing a market downturn early in retirement—known as "sequence risk"—can be devastating. By combining our **Professional** withdrawal strategies with a Takaful-based safety net, we protect your principal from early depletion while preserving wealth for your family's continuity.
