# BRANDING HAIRUL 2.0

## Brand, Positioning & Commercial Principles of Hairul Anuar Bin Sulaiman

**Version:** 2.0
**Status:** Strategic Foundation

---

# 1. WHY WE EXIST

Financial decisions can affect an individual and their family for decades.

Yet many people encounter financial products before they fully understand the problem they are trying to solve, the alternatives available, or the consequences of their decisions.

**Hairul exists to change that sequence.**

We believe people should:

**Understand → Assess → Decide → Plan → Review**

before they commit to a financial solution.

Our role is not simply to recommend products.

Our role is to help people make **better financial decisions with greater clarity and confidence**.

---

# 2. OUR MISSION

To help individuals, families and business owners in Malaysia make financial decisions with greater clarity and confidence through education, thoughtful planning, and long-term professional relationships.

---

# 3. OUR VISION

To become one of Malaysia's most trusted voices in **Shariah-compliant financial planning**.

Trust is earned through:

* consistency
* education
* sound judgement
* professional conduct
* meaningful client outcomes

—not through aggressive selling or attention-seeking marketing.

---

# 4. OUR CORE BRAND IDEA

# **UNDERSTAND BEFORE YOU DECIDE.**

This is the central idea behind Hairul.

It is more than a tagline.

It is the standard by which we communicate, advise, design, publish and serve.

We help people understand:

* what risks they face
* what they are trying to achieve
* what options exist
* what trade-offs exist
* what may be suitable
* what may not be suitable
* what action makes sense next

The goal is not to remove every uncertainty.

The goal is to help people make **more informed decisions**.

---

# 5. OUR POSITIONING

## Hairul Anuar Bin Sulaiman

### Shariah Financial Planning

A calm, education-first advisory approach for individuals, families and business owners who value clarity, responsibility and long-term financial security.

### Positioning Statement

> **Hairul helps people understand their financial choices before making decisions about protection, investment and retirement.**

Our differentiation is not that we sell different products.

Our differentiation is the **quality and discipline of the decision-making process**.

---

# 6. WHAT WE BELIEVE

## Understanding comes before action.

People deserve to understand what they are considering before committing to it.

## Planning is more important than products.

Products change.

Goals change.

Circumstances change.

Good planning must adapt.

## Protection comes before accumulation.

Before attempting to grow wealth, protect the people, income and financial foundations that make that wealth possible.

## Investing is a long-term discipline.

Successful investing is rarely about predicting the perfect entry point or finding the perfect fund.

It is about suitability, consistency, patience, risk management and behaviour.

## Every recommendation must have a purpose.

Nothing should be recommended merely because it is available.

Every recommendation must connect to a genuine need, objective or risk.

## Simplicity improves decisions.

Complexity should never be used to create the appearance of expertise.

Our job is to make important concepts easier to understand without making them misleadingly simple.

# 8. OUR PRIORITY CLIENTS

We primarily work with individuals, families and business owners whose financial decisions carry meaningful long-term consequences.

Our priority includes:

### Individuals & Professionals

People seeking greater structure around protection, investment and long-term financial security.

### Families

People who need to balance protection, responsibilities, wealth accumulation and future needs.

### Business Owners

Entrepreneurs and SME owners whose business, personal wealth, family responsibilities and long-term financial decisions are closely connected.

We also remain open to clients whose financial circumstances are becoming increasingly complex.

We prioritise:

> **quality of fit, complexity of need and long-term relationship potential—not simply income, occupation or net worth.**

The brand should remain accessible even as the commercial focus becomes progressively more selective.

# 9. OUR THREE STRATEGIC PILLARS

## 01 — PROTECT

### Family Protection & Takaful Planning

Helping individuals and families understand financial risks related to:

* death
* medical needs
* critical illness
* loss of income
* family responsibilities
* financial obligations

The objective is not maximum coverage.

The objective is **appropriate protection for the actual need**.

---

## 02 — GROW

### Shariah Investment Planning

Helping clients understand:

* investment objectives
* time horizon
* risk
* portfolio structure
* Shariah considerations
* investing behaviour
* consistency
* long-term wealth accumulation

The objective is not to chase performance.

The objective is to build a disciplined investment strategy aligned with the client's circumstances.

Investment solutions may include long-term investments as well as appropriate solutions for capital with shorter-term or liquidity-oriented purposes, subject to suitability.

---

## 03 — PREPARE

### Retirement Planning

Helping individuals think realistically about:

* retirement goals
* required income
* accumulated assets
* future expenses
* inflation
* longevity
* investment strategy
* income sustainability

The objective is not to produce an impressive retirement number.

The objective is to determine whether the plan is **realistic, sustainable and aligned with the desired life after work**.

# 10. THE HAIRUL ADVISORY PROCESS

Every meaningful client engagement should broadly follow:

## Understand

What matters to the client?

## Assess

What is the current financial position, risk and gap?

## Challenge

What assumptions may be incomplete, unrealistic or misunderstood?

## Prioritise

What should be addressed first?

## Decide

What course of action makes sense?

## Implement

Put appropriate solutions into place.

## Review

Adjust as circumstances change.

This process is more important than any individual financial product.

---

# 11. THE ROLE OF HAIRUL

Hairul is not positioned as:

* a financial guru
* an influencer
* a market forecaster
* a product salesman

Hairul is positioned as:

> **A professional adviser who helps people understand, assess and make financial decisions.**

The individual adviser should be visible enough to establish human trust while keeping the focus on the client's decisions.

### Guiding Principle

> **Show ideas first. Introduce the adviser when human trust matters.**

The brand should feel personal without becoming personality-driven.

---

# 12. OUR BRAND VOICE

We communicate like an adviser.

Our voice is:

**Calm. Clear. Respectful. Practical. Thoughtful.**

We:

* explain before recommending
* use plain Bahasa Melayu
* use established English financial terminology where it improves clarity
* avoid unnecessary jargon
* acknowledge uncertainty
* explain trade-offs
* respect the reader's intelligence
* encourage thoughtful decisions

We do not:

* manufacture urgency
* exaggerate
* make unrealistic promises
* use fear to generate enquiries
* pretend certainty where none exists
* use sensational headlines merely for clicks

---

# 13. OUR CONTENT STRATEGY

Content exists to create **understanding and trust**, not simply reach.

Our three content pillars are:

### EDUCATION

Help people understand financial concepts and decisions.

### VALUES & MINDSET

Challenge assumptions around money, responsibility, behaviour and long-term thinking.

### PROCESS & REFLECTION

Show how sound financial decisions are assessed and reviewed.

The objective is not to publish constantly.

The objective is to publish **useful thinking consistently**.

---

# 14. CONTENT-TO-COMMERCIAL PRINCIPLE

Education should not end in a dead end.

Every major content journey should answer:

> **“What is the sensible next step for someone who now understands this better?”**

The next step may be:

* continue learning
* review an existing plan
* identify a financial gap
* ask a question
* request a discussion
* seek professional advice

We remove pressure.

We do not remove direction.

---

# 15. CALL-TO-ACTION PRINCIPLE

We do not force the same CTA onto every page.

The philosophy remains consistent, but the next step should match the visitor's level of readiness.

### Early Stage

> **Fahami dahulu.**

### Consideration

> **Semak keadaan semasa anda.**

### Decision

> **Bincangkan pilihan yang sesuai dengan keperluan anda.**

### Existing Client

> **Mari semak semula pelan anda.**

The CTA should create **momentum without pressure**.

# 16. CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLES

## Calm Before Clever

The website should feel calm rather than exciting.

Visitors should feel:

* comfortable
* respected
* informed

## Education Before Persuasion

Teach first.

Recommend later.

## Simplicity Wins

When two designs communicate equally well, choose the simpler one.

## White Space Is Content

Whitespace is intentional.

Do not fill space simply because it exists.

## Typography Is the Primary Design Element

Visual hierarchy should primarily come from:

* typography
* spacing
* scale
* alignment
* structure

—not excessive colour or decoration.

## Show Ideas First

Use:

* landscapes
* architecture
* nature
* light
* texture
* meaningful objects

where they genuinely support the message.

Avoid generic:

* handshakes
* smiling sales teams
* fake office meetings
* obviously staged corporate stock imagery

Human photography may be used when it strengthens credibility or trust.

## Premium Means Restraint

Premium is achieved through discipline.

**Less, but better.**

## Timelessness Is the Goal

Design should remain credible years from now.

Do not chase trends simply because they are currently popular.

# 17. IMAGE PHILOSOPHY

Images should communicate ideas rather than merely decorate pages.

An image should have a reason to exist.

We prefer visuals that communicate:

* protection
* direction
* preparation
* clarity
* patience
* progress
* responsibility
* long-term thinking

Alt text should be natural, objective Bahasa Melayu.

Example:

> "Laluan yang tenang di hutan hujan Malaysia pada awal pagi, dengan kabus nipis dan cahaya matahari yang menembusi celah pepohon."

---

# 18. PERFORMANCE & ACCESSIBILITY

Speed contributes to trust.

Therefore:

* minimise unnecessary JavaScript
* optimise image sizes
* keep pages lightweight
* maintain readable typography
* ensure sufficient colour contrast
* support keyboard navigation
* use meaningful headings
* write useful alt text

Performance is not merely technical hygiene.

It is part of the client experience.

# 19. COMMERCIAL PRINCIPLES

We recognise an important truth:

> **Trust is the brand asset. Sustainable business is the commercial outcome.**

Being non-salesy does not mean being commercially passive.

We will build a clear path:

**Education → Recognition → Trust → Conversation → Advice → Client Outcome → Relationship → Referral**

Our marketing should create qualified opportunities without sacrificing the brand's integrity.

---

# 20. COMMERCIAL SUCCESS FRAMEWORK

Success is not measured by vanity metrics alone.

We use four levels.

## LEVEL 1 — ATTENTION

Measure whether the right people are discovering the brand.

Examples:

* relevant website traffic
* LinkedIn reach
* Google Business Profile discovery
* branded searches
* returning visitors

These are indicators, not objectives.

---

## LEVEL 2 — ENGAGEMENT

Measure whether people find the material useful.

Examples:

* meaningful content engagement
* article reads
* repeat visitors
* WhatsApp enquiries
* questions received
* consultation-page visits
* content shares by relevant people

The quality of engagement matters more than volume.

---

## LEVEL 3 — CONVERSATION

Measure whether trust is translating into real financial discussions.

Examples:

* qualified enquiries
* consultation requests
* financial reviews initiated
* referred prospects
* discovery conversations
* proposals presented
* follow-up conversations

### Key Conversion Metrics

**Visitor → Enquiry**

**Enquiry → Conversation**

**Conversation → Client**

These ratios should be monitored over time.

---

## LEVEL 4 — BUSINESS OUTCOME

Measure whether the advisory practice is creating sustainable value.

Examples:

* new client relationships
* client retention
* referral rate
* protection cases
* investment cases
* retirement-planning engagements
* recurring relationships
* Unit Trust AUM
* net AUM growth
* revenue
* revenue per client
* client lifetime value

Brand activity should ultimately support:

* stronger recognition

* appropriate investment relationships

AUM growth must never justify unsuitable recommendations.

# 22. THE UNIT TRUST AUM DRIVER TREE

AUM should be managed as an outcome of several underlying drivers.

### Net AUM

**New AUM + Existing-Client Additions + Market Movement − Outflows**

### New AUM

**Funded Clients × Average Initial AUM**

### Funded Clients

**Qualified Conversations × Conversion Rate × Funding Rate**

Therefore:

> **RM10m AUM is not a single KPI to chase. It is the output of a commercial system.**

Management should identify which driver is currently constraining growth.

# 23. LEADING AND LAGGING METRICS

| Type       | Examples                                           | Purpose                           |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Leading    | Relevant reach, qualified conversations, referrals | Predict future business           |
| Conversion | Conversation-to-client, proposal-to-client         | Diagnose commercial effectiveness |
| Client     | Retention, additional AUM, review completion       | Measure relationship quality      |
| Business   | Revenue, new AUM, net AUM                          | Measure economic outcome          |
| Strategic  | Referral rate, client quality, source economics    | Measure sustainability            |
| Activity   | Content published, outreach completed              | Measure execution only            |

### Principle

> **Do not optimise an upstream metric if it damages a downstream outcome.**

More enquiries are not automatically better.

More clients are not automatically better.

More AUM is not automatically better.

**Better-fit, sustainable client relationships are better.**

# 25. CLIENT QUALITY

Client acquisition should be evaluated using more than volume.

Important dimensions include:

* clarity of financial need
* financial complexity
* suitability
* decision readiness
* relationship potential
* capacity to invest where appropriate
* potential for long-term engagement

We should prioritise **fit and long-term value**, not simply the size of the immediate transaction.

---

# 26. REFERRAL ECONOMICS

Referrals are an important trust indicator.

Track:

### Referral Rate

**Clients generating at least one referral ÷ Active Clients**

### Referral Conversion

**Referred Clients Acquired ÷ Referred Prospects**

### Referral AUM

**AUM from Referred Clients ÷ Total New AUM**

The underlying strategic question is:

> **Does client trust translate into advocacy?**

---

# 27. CLIENT COHORT THINKING

Client relationships should be evaluated over time, not merely at acquisition.

For each client cohort, where practical, monitor:

* initial AUM
* additional AUM
* withdrawals
* retention
* referrals
* revenue
* relationship depth

A smaller initial relationship that compounds over several years may be economically more valuable than a larger one-off transaction.

---

# 28. COMMERCIAL INTEGRITY METRICS

Commercial targets must never create incentives that compromise suitability, trust or professional judgement.

Monitor indicators such as:

* client retention
* complaints
* cancellations
* early exits
* suitability concerns
* unresolved client issues
* inappropriate-fit cases

The objective is not to produce perfect-looking statistics.

The objective is to ensure:

> **Growth is not being purchased by destroying trust.**

---

# 29. BRAND HEALTH

Brand health should be evaluated periodically.

### Recognition

Do people know what Hairul does?

### Differentiation

Can they explain what Hairul does differently?

### Trust

Would they feel comfortable asking Hairul a financial question?

### Relevance

Does Hairul come to mind when they face a financial decision?

### Advocacy

Would existing clients recommend Hairul to someone they care about?

The ultimate test:

> **“Who would you speak to before making this financial decision?”**

Hairul should increasingly become one of the names that comes to mind.

We will not mistake activity for impact.

In particular:

* number of posts
* followers
* impressions
* likes
* generic website traffic
* clicks without intent

are not primary business objectives.

They are diagnostic indicators.

> **Content output is an input, not an outcome.**

# 32. THE HAIRUL COMMERCIAL BALANCE

We deliberately balance two principles:

### Remove pressure.

People should never feel manipulated into making a financial decision.

### Create momentum.

People should still know what a sensible next step could be.

Therefore:

> **Calm does not mean passive.**

> **Educational does not mean vague.**

> **Trustworthy does not mean invisible.**

> **Premium does not mean sterile.**

> **Non-salesy does not mean non-commercial.**

---

# 33. THE HAIRUL DECISION FILTER

Before publishing, designing, marketing or recommending anything, ask:

### 1. Does this build trust?

### 2. Does this educate?

### 3. Does this improve the person's decision?

### 4. Does this support a sustainable advisory business?

### 5. Is it genuinely necessary?

If the answer to #5 is no:

> **Do not do it merely because we can.**

# 36. LONG-TERM GOAL

Hairul should not aim to become the loudest financial adviser.

Nor the most fashionable.

Nor the most prolific.

Nor the most aggressive.

The objective is simpler:

> **Become the adviser people trust when an important financial decision needs to be understood properly.**

Build a brand people recommend because it is useful.

Build relationships because the advice is valuable.

Build a business that grows because trust compounds.

# 37. FINAL OPERATING STATEMENT

Before making any significant branding, marketing, content or commercial decision, ask:

> **Does this genuinely help the client make a better financial decision—and does it strengthen the long-term trust and sustainability of the advisory business?**

If yes, proceed.

If not, reconsider.
