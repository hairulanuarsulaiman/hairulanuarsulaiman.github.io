# Design Philosophy
Hairul Anuar Bin Sulaiman
Version 1.0

---

## Purpose

This website exists to help visitors make better financial decisions before they become clients.

It is an educational website first, and an advisory website second.

Every design decision should support clarity, trust and understanding.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Calm Before Clever

The website should feel calm, not exciting.

Visitors should feel:

• comfortable
• respected
• informed

Avoid visual noise.

---

## 2. Education Before Persuasion

Never pressure visitors.

Teach first.

Recommend later.

The goal is confidence, not urgency.

---

## 3. Simplicity Wins

If two designs communicate equally well,

always choose the simpler one.

---

## 4. White Space Is Content

White space is intentional.

It improves reading.

Never fill empty areas simply because they appear empty.

---

## 5. Typography Is The Primary Design Element

Hierarchy comes from:

• font size
• weight
• spacing

—not colour.

---

## 6. Photography Supports The Story

Images should:

• feel authentic
• feel calm
• support understanding

Avoid:

❌ sales teams
❌ exaggerated smiles
❌ handshakes
❌ fake corporate stock photos

---

## 7. One Purpose Per Section

Each section answers one question.

Do not combine multiple ideas into one section.

---

## 8. Every Page Should Tell A Story

Each page should have:

Hero

↓

Context

↓

Education

↓

Solution

↓

Next Step

---

## 9. Trust Is Earned Slowly

Trust comes from:

clarity

consistency

professionalism

—not marketing tricks.

---

## 10. Premium Means Restraint

Premium websites are not loud.

Premium websites remove unnecessary elements.

Less—but better.

---

# Colour Philosophy

Neutral backgrounds.

Soft contrast.

Minimal accent colours.

Never decorate with colour.

Use colour only to communicate.

---

# Typography Philosophy

Typography should feel like reading a high-quality magazine.

Comfortable line length.

Generous spacing.

Clear hierarchy.

Readable on mobile first.

---

# Layout Philosophy

Every page should follow the same rhythm.

Eyebrow

↓

Heading

↓

Supporting paragraph

↓

Content

↓

Reflection

↓

CTA

Consistency builds familiarity.

---

# Component Philosophy

Buttons

Simple.

Clear.

No gimmicks.

Cards

Minimal.

Soft borders.

No excessive shadows.

Icons

Only when they improve understanding.

Animations

Subtle.

Never distracting.

---

# Writing Philosophy

Write like an adviser.

Not a salesperson.

Use plain Bahasa Melayu.

Avoid jargon.

Explain before recommending.

Every sentence should help the reader understand.

---

# SEO Philosophy

SEO exists to help people discover useful information.

Never write for algorithms.

Write for people first.

---

# Accessibility Philosophy

Readable.

Fast.

Keyboard friendly.

Good colour contrast.

Meaningful alt text.

Accessible by default.

---

# Performance Philosophy

Fast websites create trust.

Avoid unnecessary JavaScript.

Optimise images.

Keep pages lightweight.

---

# Content Philosophy

Publish fewer articles.

Make every article worth reading.

Quality beats quantity.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The website should feel timeless.

If a visitor returns in five years,

the design should still feel professional.

Avoid following temporary design trends.

Timelessness is the goal.
