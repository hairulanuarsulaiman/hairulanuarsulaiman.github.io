2. Unit Trust

Your FIMM reference is very important.

It defines the role as:

Unit Trust Scheme Consultant

or

UTS Consultant

registered with FIMM.

Therefore...

Instead of writing

Unit Trust Consultant

(which is widely used but less precise),

I'd recommend using the official terminology whenever introducing your credentials.

For example:

Unit Trust Scheme Consultant (UTS Consultant)

Then, elsewhere on the website where you're speaking to a general audience, it's acceptable to shorten it to "Perunding Unit Trust" if it improves readability, provided it doesn't misrepresent your status.

3. Registered with FIMM

This wording is excellent because it comes directly from the regulator.

You can accurately say:

Berdaftar dengan Federation of Investment Managers Malaysia (FIMM).

That is factual.

No marketing spin.

4. "Licensed"

Notice the difference.

PWP avoided saying

licensed by BNM.

FIMM says

registered with FIMM.

Those are not interchangeable.

That's why precise wording matters.
